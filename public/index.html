      Recycling numbers

      Read the number, know the plastic

      Most plastic items carry a small triangle with a number from 1 to 7, usually on the bottom. It tells you exactly which plastic it is.

      1PETDrink bottles, clear food trays
      2HDPEJerrycans, milk and detergent bottles, many caps
      3PVCPipes, some packaging and cable insulation
      4LDPECarrier bags, soft wraps and films
      5PPBottle caps, food tubs, buckets, basins
      6PSDisposable cups, foam takeaway boxes
      7OtherMixed or specialty plastics

      Collected by Luther Plastics Not currently collected
